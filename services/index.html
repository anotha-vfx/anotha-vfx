What I Do

SERVICES

Freelance VFX artist and compositor available for projects worldwide.

From independent films and YouTube channels to commercial brands and studios — I bring cinematic quality visual effects, motion graphics, and polished video editing to every project, regardless of scale.

VFX Compositing

Green screen keying, rotoscoping, tracking, and seamless integration of 3D elements into live footage. Built for film, commercials, and digital content.

Motion Graphics

Animated titles, logo reveals, lower thirds, and full motion graphic sequences. Cinematic and brand-aligned, built in After Effects and Cinema 4D.

3D Visual Effects

3D modeling, rendering, particle simulations, and effects built in Blender. Explosions, destruction, environment elements, and photorealistic renders.

Color Grading

Professional color grading and finishing using DaVinci Resolve. From flat log footage to a polished cinematic grade that matches your visual intent.

Video Editing

Full post-production editing for films, YouTube, ads, and social media. Pacing, sound design, and story-driven cuts that hold attention.
